Start with your routine

Is Carmel the right fit?

Start your Carmel search with the routine you want. If you picture coffee, galleries, and a walk through the Arts and Design District, spend time there before choosing a home. If privacy and a large yard matter more than being near that activity, look beyond the district and compare the actual lots.

Make the drive part of your search

Before you narrow a Carmel home search to one neighborhood, drive from it to work and your regular errands. Try your normal departure time and your evening return. A home near a favorite destination still needs to work for the rest of your week.

The Arts and Design District and the Monon

A condo near the district, or a house to renovate?

The city describes apartments and condos around the Arts and Design District. That can be a useful starting point if you want less outdoor upkeep. For any existing house you consider, weigh the floor plan and condition against the cost of changing them. If you prefer a new build, ask which finishes and site work are included rather than comparing the advertised starting price alone.

Walk the route you would use

The Monon Greenway runs through the district, according to the city. Walk the route from a home you are considering to the places you would use, including crossings and the trip back. Being in Carmel is different from being a comfortable walk from the district.

Budget for the house behind the finishes

An updated kitchen does not answer questions about an older home’s systems or prior alterations. I can help you separate the cosmetic wish list from work that needs an inspection or a contractor’s estimate. For a new home, we can review the specification alongside your move-in budget.
