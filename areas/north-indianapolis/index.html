Living in North Indianapolis

Meridian-Kessler, Broad Ripple and Nora each have their own rhythm. Compare the streets, the older homes and the routes you would use before you narrow the list.

Schematic, not to scale

Three neighborhoods, three routines

Meridian-Kessler

Meridian-Kessler is known for tree-lined streets and older homes in a range of styles. If established architecture draws you, walk a few blocks instead of judging from listing photos, and note how each street connects to the shops and errands you would actually use.

Broad Ripple

Broad Ripple Village sits along the Monon Trail and the Central Canal, with shops and restaurants close together. Visit at the times you would really be there, including an evening and a weekend morning.

Nora

Nora, farther north around 86th Street, mixes established neighborhoods with nearby shopping. Drive the routes you would use most, at the hours you would use them, before you compare individual homes.

An older home deserves a closer look

Look behind the character

An older home may have been updated more than once. Ask what was changed, when, and whether the work was permitted, and keep the inspection focused on systems as well as finishes. I can help you sort cosmetic projects from work that needs a contractor’s estimate.

Plan for the house you will live with

Compare the floor plan, storage, parking and outdoor space with how you live now. A charming front porch does not settle whether the layout works, so we can list what you would change and roughly how large each project is before you make an offer.

Before you commit

Walk the trail and drive the routes

The Monon Trail runs north and south through this part of the city. If trail access matters, walk from the home to the nearest entrance, then drive your commute at the hours you would normally travel. Keep both trips in the comparison.

Confirm the school district for the address

Addresses on the north side can fall in different school districts, including Indianapolis Public Schools and the Metropolitan School District of Washington Township. Look up the specific address with the district before making a decision.
