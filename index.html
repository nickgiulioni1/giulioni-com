I'm an operator who builds his own tools.

My one real talent is finding the expensive problem in a business and fixing it. For ten years I did that on the commercial side of hardware companies—at Razer I ran a $150M annual budget with a team of six, and at Meta I led global sales and merchandising while Portal units grew 300% and Oculus units grew 450% during my time there. Then I built a construction business in Indianapolis to $3M in its first year, with a maintenance department covering 3,000+ doors.

Estimating was the thing that kept breaking. So I built the software to fix it, from inside the company that needed it. That's Ballpark.

I'm looking for AI product, solutions, enablement, and forward-deployed work where the customer is doing physical work. Construction and field service are where I know the job, but the pattern travels.

Remote, or Indianapolis. nick@giulioni.com
