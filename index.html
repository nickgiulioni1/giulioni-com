Why work with me

I've been on the owner's side of the table.

I've owned Indiana rentals since 2016, first as a long-distance investor and then on the ground in Indianapolis. As CEO of Off Leash Construction I ran a maintenance department supporting more than 3,000 doors across Greater Indianapolis, so I've seen what good management looks like from the repair side too.

Before real estate I spent ten years on the commercial side of Razer, Corsair and Meta. At T&H I put all of that to work for owners: finding the right management for each property, and bringing investors together to learn from each other.
